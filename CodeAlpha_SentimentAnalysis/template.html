Model fed the emoticons
too good to be true
Why the highest score is the one to distrust. These tweets were labelled by the emoticon the author typed — :) meant positive, :( meant negative. Leave those emoticons in the text and the classifier scores almost perfectly, because it is reading the answer key rather than the language. Strip them out and the honest score appears. That gap is the whole finding of this project.
